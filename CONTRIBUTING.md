# Contributing

Agent Office is developed and maintained by Mitchell Scholte only. This repository does not accept outside contributions:

- **Pull requests** from anyone other than the owner are closed automatically.
- **Commits and merges** are limited to the owner.

You're welcome to use Agent Office under the terms of its [license](LICENSE) (FSL-1.1-MIT). That includes changing it for your own use, but not selling it or releasing a competing copy.
